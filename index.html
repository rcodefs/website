Process

A short loop you can see

No black boxes and no status reports that say "in progress". You see working software at the end of every sprint.

  1. 01

    Discover

    We map the problem, the users and the constraints, then agree on scope and success criteria before writing code.

  2. 02

    Design

    Architecture, data model and interface decisions documented up front, so nothing important is decided by accident.

  3. 03

    Build

    Weekly demos on a working environment. Small reviewable changes, tests where they pay for themselves, no drama.

  4. 04

    Ship and support

    Deploy, monitor and hand over. Repositories, cloud accounts and documentation stay yours.
